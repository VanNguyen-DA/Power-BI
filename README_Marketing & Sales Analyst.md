## I. Introduction

### 1. Introduction to Dataset

This project analyzes a large-scale sales and digital marketing dataset containing:

- Daily revenue, orders, and ads spend  
- Campaign-level performance (impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, CPM, comments/inbox, ROAS)  
- Product-level metrics (orders, impressions, click-through, cost efficiency)  
- Customer segmentation (new vs. returning customers, membership tiers)  
- Geographical performance (top cities by revenue)  
- Budget allocation and usage efficiency  

### 2. Data Dictionary

A summarized dictionary of the main fields:

- **Date** — Daily transaction and ads performance  
- **Campaign Name** — Marketing campaign identifier  
- **Category / Product Name** — Product or product group promoted  
- **Total Revenue** — Total sales generated  
- **Ads Spend** — Daily advertising cost  
- **Total Budget / %Used** — Budget allocation and usage  
- **Impressions** — Number of times ads were displayed  
- **Clicks / CTR** — Customer engagement with ads  
- **CPC / CPM** — Cost efficiency metrics  
- **Comments + Inbox** — Customer interactions  
- **Orders** — Number of purchases  
- **ROAS** — Return on ad spend  
- **Customer Type** — New vs. returning  
- **Membership Tier** — Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond  
- **City** — Geographic sales distribution  

### 3. Business Questions

This dashboard answers key commercial and marketing questions:

#### Revenue & Ads Efficiency
- What is total revenue and how much comes from ads vs. direct sales?
- Is ads spend generating profitable returns?
- Which days have overspending or low ROAS?

#### Customer Insights
- What is the ratio of new vs. returning customers?
- Which membership tiers generate the most orders?

#### Campaign Performance
- Which campaigns deliver the highest impressions, clicks, and sales?
- Which campaigns exceed budget allocation?
- Which campaigns have the strongest ROAS?

#### Product Performance
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which products have the highest order rate?
- Which product ads are most cost-efficient?

#### Geographical Insights
- Which cities generate the highest revenue?
- Where should marketing budget be prioritized?

## II. Design Thinking Method

### Step 1: Empathize
Marketing and business stakeholders require a centralized dashboard to monitor campaign efficiency, customer behavior, product performance, and budget usage. They need actionable insights to optimize spending and maximize revenue.

### Step 2: Define
Core problem: Marketing teams lack a real-time performance dashboard that consolidates campaign data, highlights inefficiencies, and identifies high-performing products and customer segments.

### Step 3: Ideate
Potential solutions:
- Multi-page Power BI dashboard  
- Campaign-level drilldowns  
- Product performance matrix  
- Customer segmentation visuals  
- Budget vs. spend monitoring  
- ROAS and cost-efficiency KPIs  
- Geographic revenue heatmap  

### Step 4: Prototype
The first version of the dashboard includes:
- Overview page with KPIs  
- Sales & Customer page  
- Campaign performance page  
- Product performance page  
- Recommendation & Insights page  

### Step 5: Review
Stakeholders validated:
- KPI definitions  
- Definitions for ROAS, CPC, CPM, CTR  
- Visual clarity and decision-making usability  
- Color themes and layout consistency
